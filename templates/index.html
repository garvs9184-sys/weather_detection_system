Machine Learning Architecture & Physics Logic

1. Preprocessing Pipeline

Numerical features undergo standardized z-score feature scaling via StandardScaler to normalize vastly differing dimensional scales (pressure at ~1013 hPa vs visibility at ~10 km).

2. Random Forest Classification

Constructed with 120 de-correlated decision trees (max depth 16) utilizing bootstrapped bagging and random feature subspaces. Decision thresholds dynamically isolate non-linear weather phenomena such as dew point saturation for fog and barometric pressure drops for severe storms.

3. Calibration & Probability Estimation

Calculates normalized class vote frequencies across all decision trees to provide genuine probabilistic confidence scores for each distinct weather classification.
